# Neon Nexus

A Web3 social platform built around sovereign identity and decentralized networking.

## Overview

Neon Nexus is a next-generation social platform that puts users in control of their digital identity. Built on decentralized principles, it enables social interactions without the risk of platform lock-in or data siloing.
